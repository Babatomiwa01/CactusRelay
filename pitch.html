From a raccoon to a researchable venture

Lessons Learned

  • A playful story can reveal a practical coordination problem.
  • Global evidence cannot establish local customer demand.
  • A narrow segment makes a pilot easier to interpret.
  • Completed handoffs matter more than marketplace signups.
  • Buyer collection shifts cost; it does not remove it.
  • Clear assumptions make an investor model credible.
  • A polished prototype still needs real-world validation.
