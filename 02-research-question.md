# Research question

Derived from Anya’s urgent tomato rescue: among independent fresh-produce retailers in Kazan, Russia, does a lot-level, same-day surplus reservation and pickup service, compared with ad hoc phone coordination, reduce kilograms of edible tomatoes discarded per shop-week over an eight-week pilot without increasing staff handling time beyond five minutes per day?

Primary outcome: edible tomato discard weight. Secondary outcomes: recovered revenue, completed pickups, recipient acceptance, staff minutes. Proposed design: two-week baseline followed by six intervention weeks with randomised crossover where feasible; record incoming stock, temperature, prices and deliveries. A small feasibility study cannot establish population-wide efficacy. The cactus inspires resource efficiency; the raccoon and running inspire urgency, not wildlife handling.
